# Legal wording review

Files to hand to the reviewing lawyer.

- `trustiq-legal-wording-review.docx`: every piece of standard legal wording in the app (terms, privacy, retention, fair use, generated letters and instruments, e-signature, invoices, client messages), each with where it appears, the wording and what to check. Calibri, English and Arabic.
- `trustiq-legal-wording-review.md`: the same content in Markdown.
- `terms-section-6-draft.md`: the proposed Terms additions for seats, annual billing, the renewal grace period and Enterprise fair use. Not live until approved.

Notes for the reader:

- Items marked "working copy, not yet released" in the review were written while the firm pricing release was in progress. The Terms additions they mention are in `terms-section-6-draft.md` and are not part of the live Terms.
- The rule that support staff cannot use AI applies only to support staff seats bought with a new firm subscription. Staff users of existing firms keep their current access.
- Production changes need the owner's written approval.
