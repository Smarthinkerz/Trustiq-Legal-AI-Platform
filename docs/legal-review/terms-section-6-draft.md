# Terms of Service, section 6: draft additions pending lawyer review

Status: DRAFT, not live. These paragraphs were briefly published with the firm pricing release on October 15, 2026 and were taken back out of the live Terms until a lawyer has reviewed them. The live section 6 is the original text below.

## Live text (unchanged)

New workspaces receive a free trial. After the trial, continued write access requires a paid plan. Fees, plan limits and billing terms are set out in the applicable order form or pricing page. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes, including VAT.

## Proposed additions (to follow the live text)

1. Plan limits are shared across all users of a workspace. Each plan includes the number of lawyer seats paid for; support staff seats give limited access (viewing, uploading and organizing, without AI features). With annual billing the Customer pays for ten months and receives twelve.

2. If a renewal payment fails, the Customer keeps full access for seven days. After that the workspace becomes read only, with all data kept, until the subscription is renewed.

3. Fair use: where a plan is described as unlimited (Enterprise), use is subject to a fair use level of 400 AI requests and 80 new documents per lawyer per month, and at least 10,000 AI requests and 2,000 new documents per month for the workspace. We will contact the Customer if use regularly exceeds this level to agree a suitable arrangement; we will not suspend or restrict the Service automatically because of it.

## Questions for the reviewing lawyer

- Do these additions need notice to existing customers before they take effect, and how much?
- Paragraph 1 applies the "no AI" limit only to support staff seats bought with a firm subscription; firms that already had staff users keep their current access. Should the Terms say so expressly?
- Section 10 (Termination) says data is deleted 30 days after termination, while the app keeps closed matter files for 10 years by default. Which should apply, and how should the two be reconciled?
