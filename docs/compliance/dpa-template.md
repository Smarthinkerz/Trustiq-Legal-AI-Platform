# Data processing agreement (draft template)

Between **[●: firm name]** (the "Controller") and **SmarThinkerz [●: legal entity, CR number, address]**, operator of TrustiqLegal (the "Processor"). This agreement forms part of the TrustiqLegal terms of service.

## 1. Subject and duration
1.1 The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller to provide the TrustiqLegal service, as described in the record of processing, for as long as the Controller uses the service and until the data is returned or deleted under clause 9.

## 2. Instructions
2.1 The Processor processes personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions, including these terms and the Controller's use of the service's settings. It tells the Controller if it believes an instruction breaks the law.

## 3. Confidentiality
3.1 Everyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality. The Processor recognises that the data may be covered by legal professional privilege and the advocates' duty of secrecy.

## 4. Security
4.1 The Processor maintains the measures in the record of processing, including encryption in transit, encryption of stored secrets, per-firm isolation, role-based access, audit logging, backups and tested restores, and reviews them at least yearly.

## 5. Sub-processors
5.1 The Controller authorises the sub-processors listed in the sub-processor list. The Processor gives at least 30 days' notice of a new or replaced sub-processor; the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds and, if no solution is found, end the service for the affected part without penalty.
5.2 The Processor imposes data protection terms on each sub-processor at least as protective as these and remains responsible for them.

## 7. Assistance
7.1 The Processor helps the Controller answer data subject requests (export, correction, deletion tools in the service, and support within [●] days), carry out impact assessments, and consult the authority where required.

## 8. Breaches
8.1 The Processor notifies the Controller without undue delay, and in any case within [●: 24] hours of becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's data, with the information in the breach response runbook, so the Controller can meet its own deadline.

## 9. End of the service
9.1 On termination, the Controller can export its data. The Processor deletes the Controller's data within [●: 90] days unless the law requires it to keep it; data in backups is deleted when the backups expire under the retention schedule.

## 10. Audits
10.1 The Processor makes available the information needed to show compliance, including certifications when obtained (ISO 27001 planned), and allows one audit a year on reasonable notice, at the Controller's cost, subject to confidentiality.

## 11. AI processing
11.1 AI output is assistance for lawyer review and is not legal advice. The Processor logs each AI use with its sources and check results so the Controller can show how AI output was verified.
