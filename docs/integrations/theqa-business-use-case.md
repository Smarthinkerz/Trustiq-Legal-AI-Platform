<h3>3. Purpose of this request</h3>
<p>We ask to join the following THEQA services as a private entity, and to receive the technical integration documents, test environment and integration keys:</p>
<ul><li>Identity portal (unified sign-in): lawyers, staff and clients sign in to the platform with the THEQA app or their ID card.</li><li>Client identity verification: verifying a new client's identity when a firm opens their file, instead of uploading a photo of the ID card.</li><li>Certified electronic signature: signing legal documents electronically through the digital signature portal.</li><li>Signature verification: checking the validity of digital signatures on documents a firm receives.</li></ul>
<h3>4. Detailed use cases</h3>
<h4>4.1 Sign-in</h4>
<ul><li>The user chooses "Sign in with THEQA" on the sign-in page.</li><li>They approve the request through the push notification in the THEQA app or by scanning a QR code, then enter their PIN.</li><li>The platform links the identity to the user's existing account in their firm; it does not create accounts automatically.</li><li>At least the medium verification level is used.</li></ul>
<h4>4.2 Client identity verification</h4>
<ul><li>When opening a new client file, the firm sends the client a link to verify their identity with THEQA.</li><li>The platform stores the result, its time, and only the data the client agrees to share, in the firm's know-your-client record.</li><li>This supports firms' obligations to verify their clients' identity.</li></ul>
<h4>4.3 Certified electronic signature</h4>
<ul><li>Example documents: fee agreements and engagement letters, settlement agreements, client acknowledgments and internal minutes.</li><li>The lawyer sends the document for signature from the platform, and the signer receives a signing request in the THEQA app.</li><li>Signing requires the signer's identity at the high verification level, as THEQA requires.</li><li>The platform keeps the signed document and its verification result in the document's record.</li><li>The service will not be used for documents the law requires to be notarized or authenticated by another authority, except as the regulations allow.</li></ul>
<h4>4.4 Verifying signed documents</h4>
<ul><li>A lawyer uploads a digitally signed document received from another party; the platform checks the signature and certificate status through the verification service and shows the result.</li></ul>

<h3>6. Technical requirements</h3>
<ul><li>Application type: a web application used in desktop and mobile browsers, running on our servers (Node.js and a PostgreSQL database).</li><li>Sign-in: we prefer OpenID Connect or OAuth 2.0, or SAML 2.0, with push notification and QR code support.</li><li>Signing: an API to submit a signing request and receive the result through a callback; we prefer a PAdES signed PDF with a time stamp.</li><li>Verification: an API to verify a signature and certificate status (OCSP).</li><li>A sandbox and test accounts before moving to production.</li><li>We will meet any integration requirements the Ministry sets, such as server certificates, IP allowlisting or mutual TLS.</li></ul>
<h3>7. Data protection and information security</h3>
<ul><li>We comply with the Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) and its executive regulation, and the Electronic Transactions Law (Royal Decree 39/2025).</li><li>Users give explicit consent before any THEQA data is shared, and data is used only for the stated purpose.</li><li>All connections are encrypted with TLS; integration keys and secrets are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).</li><li>Role-based access, an audit log of sign-ins, changes and downloads, and two-step verification for users.</li><li>Hosting: the platform is currently hosted with a cloud provider outside Oman, and we plan to move Omani customers' data to a hosting provider inside the Sultanate. Please tell us whether hosting in Oman is a condition for THEQA integration.</li><li>We are ready to complete any security assessment or penetration test the Ministry requires before production.</li></ul>
